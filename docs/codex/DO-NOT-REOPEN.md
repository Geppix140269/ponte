# Do not reopen casually

These decisions may change only through an explicit owner decision recorded in `DECISION-LOG.md`.

- Do not turn Ponte into a generic consumer buy/sell marketplace.
- Do not merge Qualified Opportunities and Market Signals into one ambiguous feed.
- Do not publish third-party signal identity, contacts, source URLs, copied prose or private provenance.
- Do not fabricate traction, liquidity, urgency, verification or transaction activity.
- Do not expose contact details before the controlled-introduction state permits disclosure.
- Do not require registration at the entrance merely to browse or begin useful work.
- Do not lose user intent or entered work across authentication.
- Do not allow AI to auto-publish, auto-verify, auto-contact, auto-pay or make an external commercial commitment.
- Do not use a universal Trust Score or unexplained verification badge as a substitute for evidence.
- Do not use gold as a status colour for approval, warning, verification or review.
- Do not globally repaint legacy screens before their connected journey and lifecycle are approved.
- Do not treat a prototype as authority for permissions, schema, lifecycle or external action.
- Do not rewrite or replay the production migration chain without a dedicated reconciliation plan.
- Do not treat the visible live site as proof of what is in `main`, or code in `main` as proof that it is deployed.
