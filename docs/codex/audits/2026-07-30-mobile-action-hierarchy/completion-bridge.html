The universal Task Completion Bridge

The approved Task Completion Bridge (Bridge System v1, component 3) applied to Start a Deal, bound to lib/ponte/progress.ts. A gold signal travels the deck as the record fills in — gold in its lawful meaning: movement across a Bridge and arrival. Blue stays on the actions. Percentage appears only after the first meaningful act; before that the Bridge is the neutral null state. 100% = all required applicable details complete — never verified, trustworthy, low-risk or likely to close.

Three families, both sides supported, each with its own applicable field set and weight map. Seven states each, at 390×844. One desktop lead-Bridge below. Weights sum to 100, are irregular by law, and required > optional.
