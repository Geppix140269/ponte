### The acceptance criteria, each as it was actually checked

- **All six presented choices reachable, each writing the mapped intent.**
  Walked all six. `seek_brands_or_products_to_represent`, the value Set 2's own
  `B01` cannot reach, resolves.
- **The position question only in the distribution branch.** Asked by
  `resolveIntent`, not by a family test in the component; pinned by test.
- **One click, visible state change inside 100ms, in a preview environment.**
  `useTransition` on every surface; the step counter is replaced by an
  acknowledgement while a transition runs.
- **The upload route refusing an unauthenticated session.** Rendered signed out:
  `aria-disabled="true"`, no click handler, reason stated before the tap. Both
  `B02` and `B06` ask `uploadPermitted`.
- **The exact expiry date for each of 30, 60 and 90.** Rendered: 5 September,
  5 October, 4 November 2026 from 6 August. Changing the horizon changes the
  date on screen.
- **`Checked` as the label, nothing implying comprehensive screening.** The
  perimeter statement is on every state of `B09s`, unconditionally.
- **Every surface at 360px and at desktop, both themes.** 360px: no horizontal
  overflow on any surface. Desktop: 430px frame, centred. Dark theme resolves to
  the reference's own values on the new stylesheet.
- **Signed in AND signed out.** Both halves walked. The gate signs in and
  continues from the exact node without a round trip.

## 3. Nine defects found by RENDERING, not by reading

Every one of these would have passed a plausible source test. They are listed
because the pattern matters more than the individual fixes: **a test that checks
a token is referenced is not a test that the token resolved.**

1. **Capacity asked twice.** `role` is a completion field of every family's
   procedure and reads `draft.role`, which nothing set. The screen after the
   capacity declaration asked "What is your capacity on this?" as a fact
   `NEEDED TO PUBLISH`, with the answer just given discarded.
2. **The product missing from its own listing.** "The listing so far" showed
   seven gaps and one confirmed fact and never named the product. Same class as
   `P0-5`: the member cannot tell whether their choice registered.
3. **`P0-5` itself, reappearing.** "I need something" then "A trade service"
   produced *"Tell Ponte what service you PROVIDE."* The direction was read from
   `intent === "source_product"`, true for one of the four demand-side intents.
   It now reads `MARKET_INTENTS`, which already says which side each intent is.
4. **Optional fields labelled `NEEDED TO PUBLISH`.** Notes is needed to publish
   nothing. All three family procedures already exclude it from
   `factBuckets().missing`; the surface was reading `openGaps` instead. The rule
   existed; a second one had been invented that disagreed with it.
5. **The search phrase leaking into private Notes.** The member's wording was
   written to `note`, so a private field they never wrote in held a string typed
   for another purpose, and `note ?? wording` kept the abandoned first attempt.
   The wording already survives in `resolution.originalWording`.
6. **Capacity printed twice on the preview**, once from the fact list and once
   from a row added beside it.
7. **`On requestHS 271019`.** The HS code was glued to the quantity value, and
   attached to the wrong fact. A classification belongs to the product.
8. **Validity marked `FIXED` directly above the control that changes it.**
9. **A finding reported as a service failure.** When the submit route refused a
   listing for a stated reason, the surface printed *"The checks did not finish.
   A service failure, not a finding"* immediately above the finding itself, and
   offered a retry that would be refused again.

Plus one banned string reintroduced: the "Browse categories" row carried a
rephrasing of item 10. Rephrasing a retired sentence is still using it.

## 4. Deviations from the design reference, and why

The reference is the specification. These four are places where following it
literally would have broken a rule the same reference states in its own header,
or would have drawn a control Ponte does not have.

1. **`B01` position, four options to two.** Confirmed by the owner on 6 August.
   Set 2 offers "seeking a distributor / offering to distribute / seeking an
   agent / offering to represent", which encodes direction and position together
   and resolves to two stored values, leaving
   `seek_brands_or_products_to_represent` unreachable. Distributor and agent are
   partner TYPES and belong in `distribution_partner_type_key`.
2. **Three bounded rectangles rebuilt as hairlines and tone.** The reference
   draws a dashed-rectangle drop target, a bordered file-type block and bordered
   validity pills. All three are boxes, which the reference's own header
   forbids. They are now a tone band, a mono label and choice rows. The rows
   also gain 16px of tap target, which matters more at 360px than the pill.
3. **`B08` has no password field.** Set 1 draws email + password, "Create an
   account" and "Send me a sign-in link". Ponte has none of the three: it is a
   six-digit code, a first-time and a returning address both receive one, and
   `lib/auth/use-otp.ts` documents why the link was removed. Building the
   markup literally would have drawn three controls that cannot work.
4. **Validity is rows, not pills.** Same reason as (2).

**Logged for Design.** (1) and (2) are revisions to the reference itself.

## 5. Two findings for the record

### `mandate_sighted` is the desk's column, and the brief says otherwise

The brief names `submitter_role`, `chain_depth` and `mandate_sighted` as "the
existing columns" for the capacity declaration. The production schema disagrees,
in its own comment:

> `Set by the desk only. A sighted mandate is the desk's statement, never the
> poster's claim.`

A member asserting "I hold written authority" is making a claim; the desk
sighting a mandate is a different act by a different party. Writing the member's
claim into the desk's column would promote an unverified assertion to Ponte's
own statement. **The surface does not write it.** The declaration travels as the
member's own words, ending "Not sighted by Ponte", and a test forbids the column
name appearing in the capacity surface.

### The development database could not reach its own product catalogue

`hs_codes` held two rows, seeded for the listing fixtures. The submit route
refuses any code absent from that table whenever the table is non-empty, so
every product reachable through `lib/products/catalogue.ts` was refused, and
walking the path locally ended at *"271019 is not a valid HS 2022 code"*. That
is correct behaviour against a table two rows deep and useless as a development
environment. `scripts/seed-dev.mjs` now seeds the 18 codes the committed
catalogue can assign. Production carries HS 2022 in full and is unaffected.

## 6. What was NOT done

- **The `Browse categories` route hands off to `/structure`.** The catalogue
  browse is the existing classification journey and a whole surface of its own;
  it is not one of `B01`-`B09`. The handoff carries family and intent.
- **`/structure` has not been retired.** It is still the browse destination and
  still serves the entrances that link to it. Retiring it is a separate cutover
  with its own redirect work, and doing it inside this build would have coupled
  a UI replacement to a routing migration.
- **Services and distribution are architecturally complete but lightly walked.**
  The field sets differ per family and the structure does not: both families
  render `B01`, `B01b`, `B02`, `B03`-`B05`, `B07`, `B09` and correctly skip
  `B06`. All six direction-and-family statements were checked. Neither family
  was published end to end, because the resolver is a products catalogue and
  their subjects come from the classification journey behind `/structure`.
- **`B10` and the response layer** are Set 3 and out of scope, per the order.
- **Voice degrades honestly rather than working everywhere.** The Web Speech API
  exists in Chromium and Safari and not in Firefox or most in-app browsers.
  Availability is detected and the band says which case the member is in;
  typing is always available and is never the only route.
