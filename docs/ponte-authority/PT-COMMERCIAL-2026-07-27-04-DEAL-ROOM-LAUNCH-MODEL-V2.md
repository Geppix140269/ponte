# Ponte Trade Deal Room Launch Model v2

- **Status:** Proposed for product-owner approval

- **Owner:** Giuseppe Funaro

## 1. Launch ladder

Ponte launches with four connected commercial levels:

1. **Free Market Access** — discover and publish structured Deals.
2. **Starter Deal Room** — one limited real Deal Room experience per verified organisation.
3. **Deal Room Portfolio** — subscription capacity for ongoing active Deals.
4. **Ponte Credits** — pay-as-you-go master-room activation, extensions, overflow and additional guest organisations.

Human Ponte Desk and specialist services remain separately scoped and paid.

```text
Free structured Deal
  -> Starter Deal Room
  -> Portfolio subscription or Ponte Credits
  -> optional paid agent, Ponte Desk and specialist services
```

## 2. Commercial unit

The paid value-bearing object is the **master Deal Room linked to one defined Deal**.

One master Deal Room may contain unlimited directly related private sub-rooms under paid Portfolio or credit entitlement. Sub-room creation does not consume another master-room slot.

Invited counterparties and providers participate as sponsored guests. They are not required to buy a plan merely to enter an authorised sub-room.

## 3. Free Market Access

**Price:** €0

Includes:

- browse public Market Signals and Member Opportunities;
- create and submit disciplined structured Deals;
- publish eligible Deals after applicable review gates;
- leave a Deal published without opening a room;
- express structured interest;
- receive or send a proposed room invitation;
- complete registration and Business Passport requirements.

Does not include active protected Deal Room progression unless the organisation uses its Starter entitlement or a paid entitlement.

## 4. Starter Deal Room

### Recommended launch terms

- **Price:** €0;
- **Credit card:** not required;
- **Frequency:** once per verified organisation;
- **Master Deal Rooms:** 1;
- **Active term:** 30 days from first required external principal admission;
- **Private sub-rooms:** up to 3;
- **External guest organisations:** up to 2;
- **Internal organisation users:** up to 2;
- **Named participants per external organisation:** up to 3 where operationally appropriate;
- **Core workflow:** included;
- **Basic AI recap:** included within fair-use limits;
- **Human Ponte Desk or specialist work:** not included;
- **Expiry:** read-only, with upgrade to resume.

### Included core workflow

The Starter Deal Room includes the real value loop:

- formal admission and Business Passport threshold;
- Participation Agreement and NDA acceptance;
- master-room overview;
- private sub-rooms;
- procedure proposal and agreement;
- responsibilities and next actions;
- evidence upload, review and clarification;
- conditions and blockers;
- decisions and approvals;
- named commercial stage;
- stable weighted procedural completion;
- milestones;
- basic activity recap and AI summary;
- pause, close and durable history.

The Starter experience must be capable of reaching a genuine milestone. It is not a static product tour.

### Starter activation

```text
Complete or select a structured Deal
  -> choose Starter Deal Room
  -> prepare master room and initial sub-room
  -> invite required external principal
  -> principal completes admission and NDA
  -> consume Starter entitlement
  -> begin 30-day active term
```

The Starter entitlement is not consumed when the required principal declines, the invitation expires before admission, the sponsor cancels before activation, or Ponte rejects the room before activation.

Once activated, the entitlement is used even if the parties close early or no transaction results.

### Starter upgrade triggers

Upgrade is required to:

- continue active progression after 30 days;
- open a second master Deal Room;
- create a fourth private sub-room;
- admit a third external guest organisation;
- add a third internal organisation user;
- use premium agent capabilities;
- commission Ponte Desk or specialist work.

Upgrade preserves all participants, permissions, acceptances, evidence, procedure versions, decisions, blockers, milestones and audit history.

## 5. Deal Room Portfolio subscription

### Proposed launch price

- **€149 per month**, excluding applicable tax; or
- **€1,490 per year**, excluding applicable tax.

Annual billing provides twelve months for the price of ten.

### Included capacity

- 5 concurrent active master Deal Rooms;
- unlimited directly related private sub-rooms under each master room;
- 25 concurrent external guest organisations across the portfolio;
- up to 3 named participants per external guest organisation;
- 5 internal organisation users;
- standard workflow, audit, progress and AI summary capabilities;
- no human Ponte Desk entitlement unless separately scoped.

Five subscription slots mean five active Deals, not five conversations.

### Portfolio overflow

Subscribers may use Ponte Credits for:

- temporary additional master-room capacity;
- additional external guest organisations;
- future approved premium agent, evidence, specialist or Ponte Desk add-ons.

## 6. Ponte Credits

### Proposed packs

| Pack | Price excluding tax | Effective price per credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| 100 credits | €100 | €1.00 |
| 250 credits | €225 | €0.90 |
| 500 credits | €400 | €0.80 |

Credits are consumed oldest first and expire after 24 months unless applicable law or an explicit agreement requires otherwise.

Credits are contractual service entitlements, not cryptocurrency, transferable value or a payment instrument.

### Proposed uses

- **60 credits:** activate one master Deal Room for 90 days, including unlimited related private sub-rooms and the first 2 external guest organisations;
- **20 credits:** extend one credit-funded master room for 30 days;
- **20 credits:** add one temporary Portfolio master-room slot for 30 days;
- **5 credits:** admit one additional external guest organisation for the master room's current paid term.

Creating another private sub-room does not consume credits by itself.

## 7. Guest counting rule

Ponte counts an **admitted external guest organisation**, not an invitation email or sub-room.

The following consume nothing:

- draft sub-room creation;
- invitation sending;
- corrected or resent invitations;
- declined invitations;
- invitations expiring before admission;
- people who cannot satisfy admission requirements.

Guest capacity is consumed only when the organisation's first authorised participant completes admission and enters protected progression.

## 8. Paid activation and reservation

For Portfolio and credit-funded rooms:

```text
Complete or select the Deal
  -> prepare the master room
  -> select subscription slot or credits
  -> reserve entitlement
  -> create sub-room and invite principal
  -> principal completes admission
  -> consume entitlement
  -> activate protected progression
```

The reservation is released if the principal declines, the sponsor cancels before activation, the invitation expires before admission, or Ponte rejects the room before activation.

After activation, failure of the commercial transaction does not itself create a refund. A Ponte service failure may create restoration or credit under the final policy.

## 9. Expiry and lapse

When Starter access, credit entitlement or subscription cover ends:

- no room data is deleted;
- the master room and sub-rooms become read-only after the applicable grace rules;
- existing visibility permissions remain respected;
- the sponsor may restore entitlement and resume the same room;
- the system must not silently close or erase a commercial Deal.

The proposed subscription grace period is 14 days.

## 10. Sponsorship

The master room may be sponsored by:

- the Deal owner;
- an interested counterparty;
- Ponte where authorised;
- an institution or programme.

Payment or sponsorship does not transfer ownership of the posted Deal, disclosure rights or another participant's decision authority.

An invited guest may participate without purchasing its own plan. Sponsored participation does not consume that guest organisation's own future Starter entitlement.

## 11. Human and specialist services

Starter, Portfolio and credit activation pay for the software and permitted agentic Deal Room environment, not open-ended human work.

These require a separately accepted paid scope:

- Ponte-facilitated or Ponte-managed procedure;
- founder or Ponte Desk investigation;
- inspection, logistics, compliance, legal, finance or specialist support;
- custom reporting or evidence work;
- transaction, referral, completion or success-fee arrangements.

## 12. Guardrails

- Do not charge to publish a complete eligible Deal.
- Do not require every invited participant to subscribe.
- Do not make Starter a static or crippled demo.
- Do not permit unlimited repeated Starter rooms through duplicate accounts.
- Do not charge another master-room slot for sub-room creation.
- Do not describe payment as verification, safety or probability of closing.
- Do not hide limits, expiry or renewal.
- Do not delete evidence or history when entitlement ends.
- Do not include unpriced founder or Ponte Desk work.

## 13. Day-one metrics

Track:

- Starter eligibility, reservation, activation and conversion;
- time to first principal admission;
- first milestone reached;
- Starter expiry and read-only conversion;
- upgrade trigger encountered;
- conversion to Portfolio or credits;
- master rooms and sub-rooms per Deal;
- guest organisations invited and admitted;
- subscription utilisation and churn;
- credits sold, reserved, released and consumed;
- revenue per paid master room;
- Ponte Desk and specialist attachment;
- contribution margin by fulfilment mode.

## 14. Approval boundary

Approval of this document establishes the launch commercial model but does not itself authorise production charging or implementation.

Implementation still requires:

- remaining product authorities;
- entitlement and lifecycle design;
- Design and copy approval;
- schema and migration approval;
- Stripe webhook replay and idempotency safety;
- invoice, tax and legal-entity confirmation;
- customer terms, privacy and refund policy;
- tests and rollout plan;
- explicit owner approval for production activation.
